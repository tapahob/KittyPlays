KittyPlays Privacy Policy

Video processing

The KittyPlays browser extension reads video frames from the YouTube player and processes them locally in your browser to generate intermediate frames for smoother playback. Frames are held temporarily in memory during processing. The extension also checks your browser's graphics capabilities locally to select compatible features.

Saved preferences

KittyPlays saves your preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. These include whether the extension and its modes are enabled, your preferred interpolation mode, the target frame rate, and whether the FPS overlay is visible. You can change these preferences in KittyPlays. Uninstalling the extension removes its saved settings from that browser profile.

Data sharing

KittyPlays does not record your browsing history or log your activity. It does not send video frames, graphics information, or settings to external servers. It does not sell or share user data. KittyPlays uses data solely to provide its video interpolation feature and related controls, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions

For questions about the extension or this policy, use the KittyPlays issue tracker.
